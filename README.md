# Music KMM

Write your lyrics, make a melody (or use a song you downloaded), sing into the microphone, and Music KMM tunes your voice (autotune).

- **Play online:** https://kmaram16.github.io/music-kmm/
- **Windows app:** [Download](https://github.com/kmaram16/music-kmm/releases/latest/download/MusicKMM_Windows.zip)
